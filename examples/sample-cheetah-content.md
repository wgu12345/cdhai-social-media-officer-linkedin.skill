# CHEETAH 2026 Recap — Internal Notes

## Event details

- **Name**: CHITA 2026 (Conference on Health IT and Analytics)
  - Note: formerly known as CHEETAH; rebranded to CHITA starting 2025
- **Date**: April 28-29, 2026
- **Location**: Johns Hopkins Carey Business School, Washington DC campus
- **Host**: Center for Digital Health and Artificial Intelligence (CDHAI)
- **Co-Directors**: Professor Ritu Agarwal, Professor Gordon Gao
- **Attendees**: ~250 (researchers, clinicians, industry, policymakers)

## Key sessions

### Day 1 keynote
- Speaker: Harang Ju (Johns Hopkins Carey)
- Title: "AI in Clinical Decision Support: From Research to Bedside"
- Took ~45 min; followed by 20-min Q&A
- Most cited moment: discussion of incentive design across hospitals,
  payers, and patients as the harder problem behind responsible deployment

### Day 1 panel
- Title: "Equity in Clinical NLP"
- Moderator: David Sontag
- Panelists: 3 early-career researchers (names TBD — confirm with organizers)
- Audience response strong — 30+ questions

### Day 2 industry session
- "Building AI-native companies in healthcare"
- Guest: John L., cofounder & CEO of SZNS Solutions
- Class-style format, 60 min, packed room

## Notable moments

- Andrew Burton-Jones (newly joining Carey faculty fall 2026) sat in on
  the panel and asked a question about cross-institutional data sharing
- Hallway conversations were called out by multiple attendees as
  particularly valuable
- One attendee tweet (publicly posted, not yet verified for use):
