## Hashtag policy (LinkedIn-specific)

Carey's website policy is 3 hashtags. That applies to website content.
LinkedIn 2026 best practice is **3-5**, with the algorithm research showing
1-3 hashtags receive ~14.7 likes on average and 5+ triggering spam filters.

**Skill default: 4 hashtags. Range: 3-5. Hard upper bound: 5.**

Composition (recommended):

- **1 broad** — pulls in general AI/health audience
  - `#AI`, `#HealthAI`, `#DigitalHealth`, `#HealthTech`, `#AIinMedicine`
- **1-2 niche** — pulls in topic-specific audience
  - `#ClinicalAI`, `#MedicalAI`, `#HealthEquity`, `#AIEthics`,
    `#GenAI`, `#LLMsInHealth`, `#ResponsibleAI`
- **1 branded or event-specific** — community signal
  - `#CDHAI`, `#JHUCarey`, `#JohnsHopkins`, `#CHITA2026`, `#SitHigh`,
    `#MSISAI`

### Placement

- All hashtags at the **end** of the post, after the main content
- **Never in the hook** (first 2 lines)
- One blank line between body and hashtags

### Capitalization

Use PascalCase or camelCase, not all lowercase:
- ✅ `#DigitalHealth` `#AIinMedicine`
- ❌ `#digitalhealth` `#aiinmedicine`

Exception: MIT Jameel uses all-lowercase camelCase (`#aiforgood
#aiforhealth`). CDHAI defaults to PascalCase for readability.

## Image standards

- LinkedIn 1:1 (square) for inline image: 1080×1080 px minimum
- LinkedIn 1.91:1 (landscape) for banner: 1200×627 px minimum  
- Always 2x for retina: aim for 2160×2160 and 2400×1254 respectively
- Format: JPEG preferred, PNG OK for screenshots/diagrams
- Color: Carey navy `#002D72` for institutional accents
- Logo placement: bottom-right watermark, 15% opacity for non-Carey photos;
  full opacity for institutional graphics
